# Control 9

Control 9 is one of the 18 Controls of the CIS Controls, Version 8.1. CIS's guide, at its v8.1.2 revision, lists 7 Safeguards under it, numbered 9.1 to 9.7.[^cis-v8-1] The schema uses those numbers as their identifiers. Of the Safeguards, 2 are in Implementation Group 1, 6 in Implementation Group 2 and all 7 in Implementation Group 3. Its Safeguards are about the asset classes Network (4), Software (2) and Devices (1).

This bundle leaves out the Control's name, its description and the text of its Safeguards, because CIS licenses that text under CC BY-NC-ND 4.0. CIS's guide gives them, and [the guide's reference](../references/cis-controls-guide.md) says where to find it.
