# Control 9 (CIS-9)

Control 9 is one of the 18 Controls of the CIS Controls, Version 8.1. CIS's guide, at its v8.1.2 revision, lists 7 Safeguards under it, numbered 9.1 to 9.7, which the schema names `CIS-9.1` to `CIS-9.7`.[^cis-v8-1] Of these, 2 are in Implementation Group 1, 6 in Implementation Group 2 and all 7 in Implementation Group 3. Its Safeguards are about the asset classes Network (4), Software (2) and Devices (1).
