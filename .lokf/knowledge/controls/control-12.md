# Control 12 (CIS-12)

Control 12 is one of the 18 Controls of the CIS Controls, Version 8.1. CIS's guide, at its v8.1.2 revision, lists 8 Safeguards under it, numbered 12.1 to 12.8, which the schema names `CIS-12.1` to `CIS-12.8`.[^cis-v8-1] Of these, 1 is in Implementation Group 1, 7 in Implementation Group 2 and all 8 in Implementation Group 3. Its Safeguards are about the asset classes Network (5), Devices (2) and Documentation (1).

This bundle carries none of CIS's text, which CIS licenses under CC BY-NC-ND 4.0, so the Control's name, its description and the text of its Safeguards are left out. CIS's guide gives them; [the guide's reference](../references/cis-controls-guide.md) says where it is.
