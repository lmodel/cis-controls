# Control 12

Control 12 is one of the 18 Controls of the CIS Controls, Version 8.1. CIS's guide, at its v8.1.2 revision, lists 8 Safeguards under it, numbered 12.1 to 12.8.[^cis-v8-1] The schema uses those numbers as their identifiers. Of the Safeguards, 1 is in Implementation Group 1, 7 in Implementation Group 2 and all 8 in Implementation Group 3. Its Safeguards are about the asset classes Network (5), Devices (2) and Documentation (1).

This bundle leaves out the Control's name, its description and the text of its Safeguards, because CIS licenses that text under CC BY-NC-ND 4.0. CIS's guide gives them, and [the guide's reference](../references/cis-controls-guide.md) says where to find it.
