# Control 4 (CIS-4)

Control 4 is one of the 18 Controls of the CIS Controls, Version 8.1. CIS's guide, at its v8.1.2 revision, lists 12 Safeguards under it, numbered 4.1 to 4.12.[^cis-v8-1] The schema names them `CIS-4.1` to `CIS-4.12`. Of these, 7 are in Implementation Group 1, 11 in Implementation Group 2 and all 12 in Implementation Group 3. Its Safeguards are about the asset classes Devices (7), Data (2), Documentation (2) and Users (1).

This bundle leaves out the Control's name, its description and the text of its Safeguards, because CIS licenses that text under CC BY-NC-ND 4.0. CIS's guide gives them, and [the guide's reference](../references/cis-controls-guide.md) says where to find it.
