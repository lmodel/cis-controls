# Control 1 (CIS-1)

Control 1 is one of the 18 Controls of the CIS Controls, Version 8.1. CIS's guide, at its v8.1.2 revision, lists 5 Safeguards under it, numbered 1.1 to 1.5, which the schema names `CIS-1.1` to `CIS-1.5`.[^cis-v8-1] Of these, 2 are in Implementation Group 1, 4 in Implementation Group 2 and all 5 in Implementation Group 3. All its Safeguards are about the asset class Devices.

This bundle carries none of CIS's text, which CIS licenses under CC BY-NC-ND 4.0, so the Control's name, its description and the text of its Safeguards are left out. CIS's guide gives them; [the guide's reference](../references/cis-controls-guide.md) says where it is.
