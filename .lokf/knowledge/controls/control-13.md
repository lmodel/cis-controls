# Control 13 (CIS-13)

Control 13 is one of the 18 Controls of the CIS Controls, Version 8.1. CIS's guide, at its v8.1.2 revision, lists 11 Safeguards under it, numbered 13.1 to 13.11.[^cis-v8-1] The schema names them `CIS-13.1` to `CIS-13.11`. Of these, none is in Implementation Group 1, 6 in Implementation Group 2 and all 11 in Implementation Group 3. Its Safeguards are about the asset classes Network (8) and Devices (3).

This bundle leaves out the Control's name, its description and the text of its Safeguards, because CIS licenses that text under CC BY-NC-ND 4.0. CIS's guide gives them, and [the guide's reference](../references/cis-controls-guide.md) says where to find it.
