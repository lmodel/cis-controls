# Control 3

Control 3 is one of the 18 Controls of the CIS Controls, Version 8.1. CIS's guide, at its v8.1.2 revision, lists 14 Safeguards under it, numbered 3.1 to 3.14.[^cis-v8-1] The schema uses those numbers as their identifiers. Of the Safeguards, 6 are in Implementation Group 1, 12 in Implementation Group 2 and all 14 in Implementation Group 3. All its Safeguards are about the asset class Data.

This bundle leaves out the Control's name, its description and the text of its Safeguards, because CIS licenses that text under CC BY-NC-ND 4.0. CIS's guide gives them, and [the guide's reference](../references/cis-controls-guide.md) says where to find it.
