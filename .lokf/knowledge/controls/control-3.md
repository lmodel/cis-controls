# Control 3 (CIS-3)

Control 3 is one of the 18 Controls of the CIS Controls, Version 8.1. CIS's guide, at its v8.1.2 revision, lists 14 Safeguards under it, numbered 3.1 to 3.14, which the schema names `CIS-3.1` to `CIS-3.14`.[^cis-v8-1] Of these, 6 are in Implementation Group 1, 12 in Implementation Group 2 and all 14 in Implementation Group 3. All its Safeguards are about the asset class Data.

This bundle carries none of CIS's text, which CIS licenses under CC BY-NC-ND 4.0, so the Control's name, its description and the text of its Safeguards are left out. CIS's guide gives them; [the guide's reference](../references/cis-controls-guide.md) says where it is.
