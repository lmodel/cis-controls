# Explanations

* [Scope, influences and mappings](scope-and-influences.md) - Why the schema models only the structure of the CIS Controls and none of CIS's text, what shaped it (only the CIS Controls, with lmodel/slsa's release pattern), what it crosses to (lmodel/oscal and lmodel/nist-csf-v2) and what it leaves out.
* [Element names and the shared namespace](element-names.md) - Why the schema's classes and enums carry the `Cis` prefix, why its identifiers are CIS's own numbers written as strings, why the enum values are upper case, and why no class, enum, type or key of the adapt copy is shared with LOKF or the nine released lmodel copies.
